# Agricultural Market Price Trend Analysis and Forecasting Using Indian Mandi Data

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing agricultural commodity prices in India using publicly available market data. It aims to identify historical price trends, compare prices across commodities and markets, understand price fluctuations, and explore forecasting techniques.

The project uses the **Daily Commodity Prices India** dataset from Kaggle and follows a structured five-week workflow covering research, data preparation, exploratory data analysis, forecasting experiments, and documentation.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyze historical agricultural commodity prices in India.
- Understand minimum, maximum, and modal price movements.
- Compare price variations across selected commodities and markets.
- Identify historical trends and potential seasonal patterns.
- Visualize price fluctuations using charts and graphs.
- Explore a basic forecasting model for a selected commodity and market, where the data supports it.
- Document the complete workflow and findings.

## 3. Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Daily Commodity Prices India

**Source:** Kaggle

**Dataset Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/khandelwalmanas/daily-commodity-prices-india

The dataset will be inspected to verify its columns, date coverage, price units, geographic details, and data quality before analysis.

**Note:** The selected dataset primarily covers market prices. Additional datasets are required to study crop yields, agricultural production, rainfall, or resource utilization.

## 5. Project Workflow

1. **Research and Planning:** Define the research objectives, select the dataset, identify key indicators, and document the methodology.
2. **Data Collection and Cleaning:** Inspect the dataset, handle missing values and duplicates, validate dates, and prepare price fields.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Calculate descriptive statistics and compare commodity and market prices.
4. **Visualization and Trend Analysis:** Create charts to examine historical price movements, fluctuations, and potential seasonal patterns.
5. **Forecasting Experiment:** Evaluate a suitable baseline model for a selected commodity-market series if sufficient data is available.
6. **Documentation:** Summarize findings, explain limitations, and organize project files.

## 9. Expected Outcomes

The project aims to develop a reproducible workflow for agricultural market price analysis. Expected outputs include cleaned data, descriptive statistics, visualizations, comparisons across selected markets and commodities, and an optional forecasting evaluation.

The findings may help explain historical price movements and highlight differences between agricultural markets. Any conclusions will be limited to what the available data supports.

## 10. Limitations

- Data quality and completeness may vary by commodity, market, and date.
- Forecasting accuracy depends on the quality and quantity of historical observations.
- Price data alone cannot establish the causes of price changes.
- Additional data sources are needed for crop production, yield, weather, or resource-use analysis.
